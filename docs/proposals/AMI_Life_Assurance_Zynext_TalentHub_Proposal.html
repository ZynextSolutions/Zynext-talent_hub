Four questions this platform is built to answer

Spreadsheets assign training. Word files look like certificates. Neither tells a supervisor, with certainty, who is current.

01

Who was assigned, and who finished?

Assign to the company, a division, a department, a team, or a person. Status moves from enrolled to in progress to completed.

02

If someone changes team, does training follow?

The organisation tree is the assignment source. When a person is moved, enrolments can be brought into line with the new seat.

03

Does last year’s pass still count?

Recertify on a clock. The old certificate can expire and the enrolment reopen so the programme is sat again.

04

Can Compliance produce the file?

Overdue reports, certificate lookup by number, and a zip of enrolments, overdue training, and the audit log.

How AMI Life would use TalentHub

One company workspace. People sign in with the organisation slug, email, and password. Authors publish programmes; managers assign them; learners complete them; certificates can be checked by number.

Learn

Assigned work, due dates, catalogue, and a player for video, documents, quizzes, classroom, and SCORM 1.2.

Studio

Modules, eight lesson types, live sessions with attendance, assessments, skills, and a publish history.

Examine

Pre-test, module quiz, final exam, or survey. Seven question types. A grading queue for short answer and essay.

Prove

Numbered, branded certificates. Public verify page. Path certificates. Recertify cycles.

Organisation tree

Assign once to a department. Managers see only their own people.

Who signs in, and what they can do

Four organisation roles. Navigation follows permission, so a learner never sees the admin desk. There is no custom role builder in this version.

Organisation admin

HR or L&D, with Compliance as needed.

  • Tree, users, CSV, SSO
  • Studio, banks, grading
  • Reports, revoke certificates

Manager

Department supervisor, placed on a department.

  • Invite employees in scope
  • Assign in that department
  • Read subtree reports only

Instructor

Product, claims, or training author.

  • Build courses they created
  • Banks, sessions, grading
  • Cannot edit the org tree

Employee

Staff and, if you enrol them, agents.

  • Learn and sit tests
  • Own certificates only
  • No one else’s file

Verify and recertify

Each certificate has a unique number. Anyone with the number can open the public verify page and download an image. Organisation admin can revoke.

Set recertify-every-N-days on the assignment. When the window ends, the live certificate can expire and the person is enrolled again. Last year’s pass does not sit on file as if it were still valid.

AMI Life sets the number prefix, logo, signature, and layout (midnight, ivory, or slate).

Reports you can open without a second tool

Analytics

Eleven views, including learning, assessments, overdue, skills, and a simple cost view in MMK or USD.

Operational reports

Enrolments, completions, progress, assessments, certificates, overdue training, activity. CSV, PDF, or Excel. Optional email schedule.

Inspection pack

One zip: enrolments, overdue training, audit log, and a manifest.

Access and records

Isolation

Every learner, course, and certificate belongs to AMI Life’s workspace. Users cannot switch company by changing a web address.

Sign-in

Password policy, lockout after ten failures, optional authenticator MFA, optional company SSO. Short access tokens and rotating refresh tokens.

Files and audit

Uploads are not left on an open folder. Changes write who did what, on which record, with a request id and IP.

Suggested introduction

1

Workshop

Half a day. Organisation tree, first mandatory programmes, who administers, certificate branding. Public company registration left off.

2

Pilot

Four to six weeks. One or two departments, three to five courses, weekly overdue review, live certificate lookup.

3

Production

User load by CSV or SSO, job schedule, mail, backups, rehearsal of the inspection pack.

4

Scale

As usage grows: shared cache, object storage for media, a load test of video and SCORM.

In the standard system, and what would be extra work

Included

  • Four organisation roles and a platform operator
  • Course Studio, assessments, banks, grading queue
  • Numbered certificates and public verify
  • Learning paths and classroom attendance
  • SCORM 1.2, OIDC, MMK and USD training cost
  • CSV user import and scheduled reports

By later agreement only

  • Extra named roles, SAML or SCIM
  • Object storage or a content network
  • A native mobile app
  • SCORM 2004 or a remote learning-record store
  • Generative authoring or machine marking

For live running, AMI Life (or the host) schedules reminder and recertify jobs and completes production mail. MFA can be required as a policy; it is optional per user in the product today.
